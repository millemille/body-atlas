Open3Dmodel peripheral nerve meshes in public/atlas/nerves.glb

Source: AnatomyTOOL Open3D project, upper limb and lower limb, July 2025.
https://anatomytool.org/open3dmodel
The limb nerves were newly modelled for Open3Dmodel (CC BY-SA 4.0).
Z-Anatomy (CC BY-SA 4.0) is the predecessor; it stored many of these paths
as curves rather than the meshes this atlas needs.

The atlas shades these meshes yellow. The ".r" side is mirrored so both limbs
carry the same named nerve. Cranial nerves in this GLB stay BodyParts3D.
